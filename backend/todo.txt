## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as a senior software architect, Laravel developer, database architect, UI/UX designer, event operations consultant, and QA engineer.

Build a complete, production-ready, enterprise-grade **Event Management Software** using Laravel, PHP, and MySQL.

The software must support different types of event management businesses, including:

- Wedding planning and management
- Engagement ceremonies
- Birthday parties
- Anniversary celebrations
- Corporate events
- Business conferences
- Seminars and workshops

- Product launches
- Award ceremonies
- Concerts and music festivals
- College festivals
- Cultural programs
- Government events
- Religious and community events
- Sports events
- Private parties
- Destination events
- Hybrid and virtual events

The application must be suitable for small event planners, professional event management agencies, large event production companies, wedding planners, venues, and multi-branch event management businesses.

Build this as a complete business management platform, not simply an event calendar or booking application.

The software must manage:

1. Lead generation and enquiries

# 39. CRITICAL BUSINESS RULES

Implement the following rules.

1. Every event must have a unique event ID within its company.
2. Every event must be linked to a valid client or authorized booking source.
3. Confirmed events must have the required booking approval.
4. Venue bookings must not overlap for the same available space.
5. Setup and dismantling periods must be included in venue availability checks.
6. Equipment must not be allocated beyond available quantities.
7. Staff members must not be assigned to conflicting shifts without authorized override.
8. Every quotation revision must preserve its previous version.
9. Approved quotations must not be silently edited.
10. Budget changes above configured limits must require approval.
11. Vendor work orders must be linked to an event or approved procurement request.
12. Vendor payments must not exceed approved payable amounts without authorization.
13. Guest check-in must prevent duplicate entry for the same ticket or guest, subject to configured re-entry rules.
14. Ticket quantities sold must never exceed available inventory.
15. Ticket cancellation and refunds must follow configured policies.
16. QR tickets must be uniquely identifiable and securely validated.
17. Financial transactions must create corresponding ledger entries.
18. Posted invoices must be corrected through credit/debit notes or reversal workflows.
19. Event cancellation must preserve payment, vendor, contract, and refund history.
20. Client portals must never expose internal costs or profit margins.
21. Vendor portals must never expose other vendors' commercial information.
22. All important approvals and changes must be logged.
23. Automated actions must be safe to retry without creating duplicate records or payments.
